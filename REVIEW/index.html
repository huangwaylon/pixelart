Skinned rig: new whole-body motion + baked spring secondary (idle, walk, run, jump)

needs-reviewswordswomanexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Does authored whole-body motion (pelvis bob/sway/rotation, counter-rotating chest, overlapping arms, IK-planted feet with heel-to-toe roll, lagging head, squash and stretch) with spring-simulated hair, bun, bow, sleeves and skirt fix the owner's verdict on the H2 animations ('stiff and unnatural, joints off, need more dynamic movement and bouncing parts')?
method
Motion files in the CONTRACT format (motion/<act>.json, 24 fps source, sprite frames with timing_ms) baked by motion/scripts/mbuild.py: analytic 2-bone leg IK on a treadmill foot track (no slide at travel_m_per_s); compass-gait pelvis height for the walk (DOWN lowest, UP highest); phase-lag overlap from shoulder to hand; head lag spring; damped particle chains for every secondary chain (gravity hang, travel drag, cone limits, head/thigh/torso collision, skirt panels driven by the thighs, settled loops with seam <= 0.4 mm). Rendered with R's render_motion.py (--preview for the boards; full pixel chain + head stamp for the GIFs). 7 critique rounds (Opus + Argon) on numbered SE/E frame grids + onion skins, then A/B against the old rejected frames. pxeval seq on the SE pixel frames.
result
walk 8×105 ms (bob 4.6 cm p-p, heel strike 30°, arms ±24–28° moving inward); run 8×70 ms (30% stance with a flight phase, lean 24°, arms −52/+62°); idle 12 frames over 2.4 s (holds 300/300, eased transfers 80/120/160, settle 240, contrapposto ±11°, head turn toward the camera); jump 16 frames one-shot (crouch hold, take-off stretch, ballistic rise, 120 ms apex, 35 ms toe landing, squash, overshoot onto the toes). pxeval walk boil .13→.06, idle pose_range .27→.46, head_range 1–4→5–7 px (the head now bobs and tilts instead of a rigid stamp). A/B old→new: walk 4→5 (Opus) / 4→5 (Argon); run 4→5 / 3.5→5.5; idle 4→5 / 3.5→5.5; both say 'partly fixed'. Absolute critic scores plateaued at 5–6 (jump Opus 6, key poses 7). Target 7 not reached. | r4 (R, 13:45): re-baked against the mirrored rig (sleeve/hair_side bones were 75-129/14 mm off symmetry, so the sleeves flared on one half of the cycle only), thicker legs, larger hands, SE yaw -15; re-rendered with final.sh. pxeval walk SE before -> after: flicker .292->.262, boil .058->.056, rigid_share .434->.355, sil_change .292->.264, motion_px 1.16->.97, head_jerk 3.7->4.5, centroid_jerk 3.9->5.6; flags unchanged (BOILING, SHADE_SWAP, FEET_JITTER). Run loop seam 3.8 mm after the re-bake. Pre-r4 files: motion/out/pre_r4/.
verdict
Needs the owner's eye: clearly livelier than the H2 frames in every A/B, but not yet game quality. Open issues: sleeves read as 'wings' on one half of the SE cycle (SE projection + asymmetric sleeve bones in the rig, reported to R); torso twist/roll too small on screen; bun/bow/skirt bounce limited by the rig's deformation caps; big boots hide heel strikes. Critics contradict each other on stride length and foot placement, so further critic rounds have low value.

Owner review of the old-vs-new GIFs. Then: R mirrors the sleeve bones; try a camera-aware SE cheat (more chest twist, less outward arm swing in diagonal views); raise the bow/skirt deformation caps if the rig allows; add E/S full pixel renders.

Skinned rig (R): one connected deformable mesh replaces the rigid-parts rig H2

promisingswordswomanexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Owner on the H2 animations: 'body parts are not connected, stiff, joints look off; need bouncing parts'. Can one skinned mesh with secondary chains fix connectivity and deformation, while also fixing the design errors (T-pose sleeves, thin obi, short bell skirt, cinched waist)?
method
rig/build_mesh.py: H2 head and bun plus procedural lofts fitted to the m96 masters (no-cinch torso, wide obi, straight skirt, bell sleeves with arms down 26 deg, thigh-highs, boots, side locks, bow and ribbons), unioned by a 0.006 voxel remesh into one shell of ~118k verts. rig/build_rig.py: 63 bones (contract names plus shoulders, no obi_tail); weights are part-aware and analytic (joint-plane partition of unity, azimuth skirt panels, sleeve bag chains, hair windows fading to 0 at the skin), Laplacian-smoothed; dual-quaternion Preserve Volume. render/render_motion.py: game camera; preview at 2x, or full 8x guide/lum/dep passes into render/pixel_chain.py (hires3d best chain celo, k-centroid, palette lock and fix, optional m96 head stamp). Iterated on SE stress poses against Opus critiques.
result
One continuous silhouette in every stress pose and in M's walk/run/idle/jump (SE/E/S). Fixed during QA: bang tear (hair weights now fade near skin), obi smear into the armpit (sleeve and obi were fused in the remesh), hands pixelizing to detached 1-2 px. Band churn SE (raw celo_kc) walk .187, run .184, idle .120, versus H2 hires3d walk .173 and idle .084. r4 (follow-up): sleeve.* and hair_side.* bones, weights and geometry mirrored exactly about x=0. Cause: the sleeve loft's back axis flipped sign per side, so the L sleeve bag sat forward (75-129 mm off); the H2 head is asymmetric. Every .L/.R pair is now mirror-symmetric (error 0). Legs thickened toward the chibi masters (knee r .050->.057, calf .051->.059, boots +12%). Hands are now a 3x3 px blob at 1x. SE yaw sweep against EC0_SE_96: 0/-10/-15/-20/-25 gives silhouette IoU .850/.863/.861/.848/.830; -15 adopted as the SE default (closest 3/4 frontality, rig/qa/yaw_SE_sweep.png). Band churn SE raw: walk .152, run .156, idle .112, jump .179.
verdict
Promising: Opus calls it 'a clear improvement'. Old H2 vs new: connectivity 3 vs 8, deformation 3 vs 6. Not shippable yet. Remaining: placeholder rig head (use --stamp); deep crouch collapses the solid skirt; flat sleeve-cuff disc on big arm raises; skirt only coupled to the legs at the hem; bangs limited to 15 deg (surface-painted).
next
Heads96 layered head that turns with the rig (replace the H2 placeholder head); corrective hip/knee helpers; drive the front/back skirt panels from thigh swing in M's sim; an open cuff (inner shell) for bell sleeves; SE yaw -15 is now the default.

Hi-res 3D → celo/k-centroid frames as a low-boil animation underlay (walk, run, idle)

rejectedswordswomanexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Is the same method run over consecutive frames (walk_SE/E/S 8 frames, run_SE 8, idle_SE 11) consistent enough to drive animation with less boiling than the hand-redrawn animate96 pilot?
method
Every method from the hires3d entry over each clip. band_churn = share of same-colour-class cells whose shade band changes between frames (computed from the sprites alone, so the pilot is measured the same way). Static-cell flicker uses render-unchanged 8×8 blocks. tlock.py pastes the previous output back on static cells.
result
walk_SE band_churn: celo_kc .17, matv .17, cel_kc .20, shade_kc .31, pilot .30. idle_SE: celo_kc .084, pilot .128. Render-static cells are only 5–57 per walk transition (<2 % of the figure), so paste-back takes flicker to .005 but barely moves churn (.365 → .357). The head stamp raises churn to .34–.37 because the rigid stamp snaps 1 px and doesn't turn.
verdict
OWNER (2026-10-28): 'body parts are not connected, the animation and poses look stiff and unnatural, the joints look off and unnatural. Need more dynamic movement, more movement details across the whole body, bouncing parts.' Root cause: the rigid-parts rig (H2) plus barely exaggerated mocap. Follow-up: skinned connected rig + authored whole-body motion + springs (2026-10-28_sw_skinned). Earlier team verdict: Promising as an underlay only. Opus rates 3D walk consistency 7 vs the pilot's ~4, but design fidelity 3.5 vs ~7. Argon: consistency 4.5, fidelity 2.5. Best chain: celo → k-centroid (or matv) → rig-material palette lock → fix. The off-model rig and the oversized stride must be fixed before this is worth feeding into masked edit-mode redraws.
next
After the rig fixes, run celo_kc over frames_M walk and use it as the underlay and moved-region mask for masked edit-mode redraws. Replace the rigid head stamp with heads96 layers that tilt and turn. Halve the walk stride.

question

method
fixlib.fix_sq = fixer detect + square-grid check by round-trip error; figure crop; native -> 96 px (nearest <12%, else k-centroid x8); pz.finish palette lock (palette.json) + lock2 (obi recovery, per-material lightness match). metrics.py (pixtools venv), s3_identity.py, Argon + Opus A/B vs the m96 master. Snowpeak: render 3D at 4x/8x, then pixelate + shipped fix chain (S4_hd2, S4_hd)

rejectedsnowpeakexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Is pixel art better if we render the same model/pose/camera at high resolution, downscale with k-centroid, RD pixel-fixer, majority vote or area average, and then run the fix pass, instead of using the native sprite-resolution pass?
method
The hi-res tracer fires the same rays as render_pixels(ss=k) and keeps a buffer per sample (k=4,8): depth, normal, lambert, material, group, band, smooth and flat RGB. An edge pass marks depth breaks and normal creases. Each method resolves to a 48x64 PixelFrame. A runtime swap of frames.render_pixels then runs the whole shipped fix chain on each one (face cels, pixelrules, sel-out outline, ring trim, bow shine, orphans, temporal_hold). Coverage: S4_hd2 idle/walk/run x 8 dirs x 8 frames; S4_hd idle/walk (run S/SE/E). Scripts in exp/2026-10-28_snowpeak_hires/scripts.
result
Native re-render = shipped (0 px diff). Orphans are 0 for every method and height deltas are unchanged. S4_hd2 idle, native vs best hi-res (KC, k=8): jaggies 0.55 vs 0.92 per frame, coat islands 0.06 vs 0.33 per frame, ABA boil 3.38 vs 2.98. Walk: jaggies 1.00 vs 1.53, ABA 53.2 vs 53.9. The pixel-fixer is worst (walk ABA 58.4; idle diff vs native 21%). k=4 and k=8 give near-identical results.
verdict
Rejected. Argon: N 6, KC 6 (same), VM 5.5, KCE 4.5, PF 3.5. Opus: N 6, KC 6 (same), VM 5, KCE 5, PF 3. Both critics: high-res fixes none of the six known problems (coat slab, mitten islands, stub legs, blob hands, small S bow, fringe ridges). These come from the model and design, not from sampling. Normal-buffer interior lines come out broken dashes that boil.
next
Keep the native pass. Put effort into the model: keep the mitten off the coat front (or add a rule against foreign islands inside the coat), a 2-tone coat with a fold line, r13 re-proportioning, a per-direction bow/fringe part library. Cheap carry-over to test: majority-band vote inside the native ss=3 pass (idle boil -9 to -12%).

pxeval: shared deterministic still + motion evaluator (boil, shade-swap, articulation, fidelity vs illustration)

promisingbothresult · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Can deterministic pixel and motion metrics replace the noisy LLM critic scores as the first filter, and give the three running experiments one comparable number set?
method
pipeline/tools/pxeval.py (numpy/scipy/pillow; byte-identical under the pixtools, pixel-art-fixer and swordswoman venvs). still = metrics.py port (ssim_b, dE, sil, face_ssim...) + vs-master + cleanliness (off-palette, qa.py orphans, render.py jaggies, outline coverage/gaps, L-corners). seq = motion-compensated change maps: boil (noisy redraw on a region that is rigid across t-1..t+1), shade_swap, articulation/s (non-rigid motion), silhouette and feet jitter, loop seam. board and gif helpers. Back-test: pipeline/tools/pxeval_backtest.py.
result
Still: equal to swordswoman metrics.py on 15 sprites x 15 keys (max diff 5e-5, which is rounding). top1 vs illustration ssim_b .943-.966, dE 7.2-9.6. The accepted m96 masters score ssim_b .75-.89 vs the raw illustration but .92-.96 vs their geo/ source, so fidelity for new work should be measured against the masters. metrics_top.json is stale (top1 was regenerated today). Motion: boil puts the rejected pilot walk at .031 and every shipped snowpeak walk/run/idle (24 clips) at .017 or below; shade_swap .030 vs .013 or below. The pilot idle is not boiling but STIFF: 0.10 articulation/s, one rigid 1 px block shift, 2 exact holds (lively walks are 0.5 /s or more). 64 px mocap/unarmed 3D-guide pixels boil at .06-.18.
verdict
Use as the pre-filter before any LLM critic. Gates: boil <= .02 and shade_swap <= .015 fail; articulation < 0.15/s and feet_range > 2 warn. Provisional: calibrated on one shipped project and one rejected pilot. Re-calibrate on the first owner-verdicted clips.
next
Run seq/still on every clip from the hires3d, snowpeak_hires and gemini_fixer experiments. Collect owner verdicts and re-fit the gates. Add --mask from the rig guides to measure static_changed. Arc metrics for tracked props (sword tip) once guides export keypoints.
