Hi-res 3D → celo/k-centroid frames as a low-boil animation underlay (walk, run, idle)

rejectedswordswomanexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Is the same method run over consecutive frames (walk_SE/E/S 8 frames, run_SE 8, idle_SE 11) consistent enough to drive animation with less boiling than the hand-redrawn animate96 pilot?
method
Every method from the hires3d entry over each clip. band_churn = share of same-colour-class cells whose shade band changes between frames (computed from the sprites alone, so the pilot is measured the same way). Static-cell flicker uses render-unchanged 8×8 blocks. tlock.py pastes the previous output back on static cells.
result
walk_SE band_churn: celo_kc .17, matv .17, cel_kc .20, shade_kc .31, pilot .30. idle_SE: celo_kc .084, pilot .128. Render-static cells are only 5–57 per walk transition (<2 % of the figure), so paste-back takes flicker to .005 but barely moves churn (.365 → .357). The head stamp raises churn to .34–.37 because the rigid stamp snaps 1 px and doesn't turn.
verdict
OWNER (2026-10-28): 'body parts are not connected, the animation and poses look stiff and unnatural, the joints look off and unnatural. Need more dynamic movement, more movement details across the whole body, bouncing parts.' Root cause: the rigid-parts rig (H2) plus barely exaggerated mocap. Follow-up: skinned connected rig + authored whole-body motion + springs (2026-10-28_sw_skinned). Earlier team verdict: Promising as an underlay only. Opus rates 3D walk consistency 7 vs the pilot's ~4, but design fidelity 3.5 vs ~7. Argon: consistency 4.5, fidelity 2.5. Best chain: celo → k-centroid (or matv) → rig-material palette lock → fix. The off-model rig and the oversized stride must be fixed before this is worth feeding into masked edit-mode redraws.
next
After the rig fixes, run celo_kc over frames_M walk and use it as the underlay and moved-region mask for masked edit-mode redraws. Replace the rigid head stamp with heads96 layers that tilt and turn. Halve the walk stride.

Hi-res 3D (rig H2 at 8x) → pixelize + fix, as a replacement for the illustration-based masters

rejectedswordswomanexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Does rendering the calibrated 3D rig at 8x (1024 px per 128 cell), then downscaling (E2 k-centroid / area / pixel-art-fixer / PixelOE / material vote) and fixing (rig-material palette lock, outline, orphans, head stamp), give better 96 px pixel art than the m96 masters?
method
Blender 5.2: mrender.py (rig H2 + springs) exec'd with an 8x resolution and extra lum/normal/depth passes. Styles: shade (existing toon), cel (locked-palette 3-band ramps from smoothed lum), celo (cel + hi-res depth/material lines), flat matid. 5 downscalers × 4 styles, each also with the m96 head stamp. Scored with metrics.py against the illustration and directly against the master. Scripts in exp/2026-10-28_sw_hires3d/scripts.
result
Every 3D candidate loses to the master in every view. SE ssim_b .38–.39 vs .67, sil .62 vs .83. E .64–.68 vs .76. S .75 vs .85. Downscaler choice moves ssim_b by ≤ .03 and style by ≤ .05. Without the head stamp the face is unusable (face_ssim ≤ .2). Best still: celo_kc_hs (Opus 4.5) or matv_hs (Argon 3.5); master = 7.
verdict
Rejected for stills. The gap is structural (T-pose sleeves, thin or missing obi, bell skirt with a bare thigh band, waist cinch, placeholder face), so no downscaler can close it. PixelOE and pixel-art-fixer add nothing over k-centroid on a known grid. See the hires3d motion entry for the animation side.
next
Keep the m96 masters as the look. To use 3D as an animation underlay, fix rig H2 first: arms-down rest, a wide lavender obi part, a longer straight skirt with the red stocking band, no waist cinch.

Gemini 'pixel art' + Retro Diffusion pixel-art-fixer: grid recovery, 96 px SE sprite

inconclusiveswordswomanexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Does pixel-art-fixer (grid recovery, 1 cell = 1 px) plus the E2 palette lock rescue Gemini pixel-art outputs, old (26 images) and new (SE illustration -> 'true pixel art ~96 px')?
method
fixlib.fix_sq = fixer detect + square-grid check by round-trip error; figure crop; native -> 96 px (nearest <12%, else k-centroid x8); pz.finish palette lock (palette.json) + lock2 (obi recovery, per-material lightness match). metrics.py (pixtools venv), s3_identity.py, Argon + Opus A/B vs the m96 master. Scripts in exp/2026-10-28_sw_gemini_fixer/scripts.
result
Fixer is exact on its fast path (8.00/5.12/10.24 px), but 15/26 baseline images went to arbitration, which usually mis-detects step_x by 2-12%. The square check won on 14/26 (round-trip error 7-10 -> ~2 on 8 px grids). Gemini never draws at the requested size: 82-347 art px figures on 3-14 px grids (scale to 96 = 0.28-1.17). The old outputs stay off-model (ssim_b .13-.26). New a1b (fixer, x0.38, lock) gives ssim_b .93 / dE 10.7 vs the illustration, against the master's .81 / 16.4 (master vs its warped geo src: .92 / 11.1). Passing the master as a reference (a2) hurt it: .74 / 18.2.
verdict
Grid tool: keep (always via fix_sq). As a master replacement: no. Both critics pick the master (face at 1x; dark seam noise, eye blobs). Port a1b's red sock tops, crossed collar and visible bun by hand if wanted.
next
Use the fixer (fix_sq) on pixel-native generator output (Retro Diffusion / PixelLab) if those are approved. Do not use Gemini as the pixel artist.

Snowpeak: render 3D at 4x/8x, then pixelate + shipped fix chain (S4_hd2, S4_hd)

rejectedsnowpeakexperiment · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Is pixel art better if we render the same model/pose/camera at high resolution, downscale with k-centroid, RD pixel-fixer, majority vote or area average, and then run the fix pass, instead of using the native sprite-resolution pass?
method
The hi-res tracer fires the same rays as render_pixels(ss=k) and keeps a buffer per sample (k=4,8): depth, normal, lambert, material, group, band, smooth and flat RGB. An edge pass marks depth breaks and normal creases. Each method resolves to a 48x64 PixelFrame. A runtime swap of frames.render_pixels then runs the whole shipped fix chain on each one (face cels, pixelrules, sel-out outline, ring trim, bow shine, orphans, temporal_hold). Coverage: S4_hd2 idle/walk/run x 8 dirs x 8 frames; S4_hd idle/walk (run S/SE/E). Scripts in exp/2026-10-28_snowpeak_hires/scripts.
result
Native re-render = shipped (0 px diff). Orphans are 0 for every method and height deltas are unchanged. S4_hd2 idle, native vs best hi-res (KC, k=8): jaggies 0.55 vs 0.92 per frame, coat islands 0.06 vs 0.33 per frame, ABA boil 3.38 vs 2.98. Walk: jaggies 1.00 vs 1.53, ABA 53.2 vs 53.9. The pixel-fixer is worst (walk ABA 58.4; idle diff vs native 21%). k=4 and k=8 give near-identical results.
verdict
Rejected. Argon: N 6, KC 6 (same), VM 5.5, KCE 4.5, PF 3.5. Opus: N 6, KC 6 (same), VM 5, KCE 5, PF 3. Both critics: high-res fixes none of the six known problems (coat slab, mitten islands, stub legs, blob hands, small S bow, fringe ridges). These come from the model and design, not from sampling. Normal-buffer interior lines come out broken dashes that boil.
next
Keep the native pass. Put effort into the model: keep the mitten off the coat front (or add a rule against foreign islands inside the coat), a 2-tone coat with a fold line, r13 re-proportioning, a per-direction bow/fringe part library. Cheap carry-over to test: majority-band vote inside the native ss=3 pass (idle boil -9 to -12%).

pxeval: shared deterministic still + motion evaluator (boil, shade-swap, articulation, fidelity vs illustration)

promisingbothresult · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Can deterministic pixel and motion metrics replace the noisy LLM critic scores as the first filter, and give the three running experiments one comparable number set?
method
pipeline/tools/pxeval.py (numpy/scipy/pillow; byte-identical under the pixtools, pixel-art-fixer and swordswoman venvs). still = metrics.py port (ssim_b, dE, sil, face_ssim...) + vs-master + cleanliness (off-palette, qa.py orphans, render.py jaggies, outline coverage/gaps, L-corners). seq = motion-compensated change maps: boil (noisy redraw on a region that is rigid across t-1..t+1), shade_swap, articulation/s (non-rigid motion), silhouette and feet jitter, loop seam. board and gif helpers. Back-test: pipeline/tools/pxeval_backtest.py.
result
Still: equal to swordswoman metrics.py on 15 sprites x 15 keys (max diff 5e-5, which is rounding). top1 vs illustration ssim_b .943-.966, dE 7.2-9.6. The accepted m96 masters score ssim_b .75-.89 vs the raw illustration but .92-.96 vs their geo/ source, so fidelity for new work should be measured against the masters. metrics_top.json is stale (top1 was regenerated today). Motion: boil puts the rejected pilot walk at .031 and every shipped snowpeak walk/run/idle (24 clips) at .017 or below; shade_swap .030 vs .013 or below. The pilot idle is not boiling but STIFF: 0.10 articulation/s, one rigid 1 px block shift, 2 exact holds (lively walks are 0.5 /s or more). 64 px mocap/unarmed 3D-guide pixels boil at .06-.18.
verdict
Use as the pre-filter before any LLM critic. Gates: boil <= .02 and shade_swap <= .015 fail; articulation < 0.15/s and feet_range > 2 warn. Provisional: calibrated on one shipped project and one rejected pilot. Re-calibrate on the first owner-verdicted clips.
next
Run seq/still on every clip from the hires3d, snowpeak_hires and gemini_fixer experiments. Collect owner verdicts and re-fit the gates. Add --mask from the rig guides to measure static_changed. Arc metrics for tracked props (sword tip) once guides export keypoints.
