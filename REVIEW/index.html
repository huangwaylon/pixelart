pxeval: shared deterministic still + motion evaluator (boil, shade-swap, articulation, fidelity vs illustration)

promisingbothresult · 2026-10-28 · #
question
Can deterministic pixel and motion metrics replace the noisy LLM critic scores as the first filter, and give the three running experiments one comparable number set?
method
pipeline/tools/pxeval.py (numpy/scipy/pillow; byte-identical under the pixtools, pixel-art-fixer and swordswoman venvs). still = metrics.py port (ssim_b, dE, sil, face_ssim...) + vs-master + cleanliness (off-palette, qa.py orphans, render.py jaggies, outline coverage/gaps, L-corners). seq = motion-compensated change maps: boil (noisy redraw on a region that is rigid across t-1..t+1), shade_swap, articulation/s (non-rigid motion), silhouette and feet jitter, loop seam. board and gif helpers. Back-test: pipeline/tools/pxeval_backtest.py.
result
Still: equal to swordswoman metrics.py on 15 sprites x 15 keys (max diff 5e-5, which is rounding). top1 vs illustration ssim_b .943-.966, dE 7.2-9.6. The accepted m96 masters score ssim_b .75-.89 vs the raw illustration but .92-.96 vs their geo/ source, so fidelity for new work should be measured against the masters. metrics_top.json is stale (top1 was regenerated today). Motion: boil puts the rejected pilot walk at .031 and every shipped snowpeak walk/run/idle (24 clips) at .017 or below; shade_swap .030 vs .013 or below. The pilot idle is not boiling but STIFF: 0.10 articulation/s, one rigid 1 px block shift, 2 exact holds (lively walks are 0.5 /s or more). 64 px mocap/unarmed 3D-guide pixels boil at .06-.18.
verdict
Use as the pre-filter before any LLM critic. Gates: boil <= .02 and shade_swap <= .015 fail; articulation < 0.15/s and feet_range > 2 warn. Provisional: calibrated on one shipped project and one rejected pilot. Re-calibrate on the first owner-verdicted clips.
