   The bottom-right chin outline breaks open, leaving floating dark pixels and detached skin pixels around the mouth. **Fix:** Manually close the 1px dark jawline border and mask any face-layer pixels extending past the profile silhouette.

Also, the right hair outline is sheared off in steep vertical steps. **Fix:** Offset the screen-right eye 1px lower than the left eye during tilt frames (`f06–f10`), and round out the right hair silhouette.

In `jump_SE` (f05–f07), the far `joyful` eye arch pokes outside the head silhouette into the raised arm. **Fix:** Trim floating hair crumbs and clip/shift the far eye 1px inward so the cheek outline contains it.

5. **`idle_SE` (f09) — Rogue Top-Of-Head Artifact:**
