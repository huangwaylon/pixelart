**Top 5 problems in B**
1. **f0–f3 and f10–f11, legs:** the legs merge into one mass in the silhouette, and the feet cross or overlap. Open the stance by 2 px and keep the front foot outward. This breaks the "never crossing" rule.
2. **f0–f4, posture:** the head and neck jut forward and the upper back rounds. Pull the head back 1–2 px over the chest and lift the chest about 8°.
3. **f5–f7, contrapposto:** both legs are straight and parallel and the hips are level. Tilt the pelvis 6–8°, shift the hips 1–2 px over the standing leg, bend the free knee, and lift that heel 1 px.
4. **f2–f10, front arm:** the hand sticks out sideways about 3 px past the sleeve, like a rigid paddle (the separated hand in the f5 silhouette shows it). Tuck it 2 px toward the hip and soften the wrist droop.
5. **All frames, secondary and holds:** the bun and bow need 1–2 frames of drag on the transfers (1 px against the head's direction, then settle). The sleeve hems need ±1 px sway. The holds need a 1 px breathing rise in the chest/shoulders so they read as alive, not paused.
