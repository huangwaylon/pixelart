Top 5 remaining problems in B:

1. **Arms, f3–f5:** both sleeves fly out sideways at the same time, about 8–10 px past the torso, almost a T-pose, so it reads as flapping. The arms should swing forward and back, opposite to the legs, by about ±4 px. They should drop to about 2–3 px flare, not hang dead straight as in f0, f1, f6 and f7.
2. **Contact poses, f0 and f4:** the stride is too narrow. At f4 the legs nearly overlap, which should never happen on a contact frame. The feet need about 6–8 px of front-to-back spread, with a straight leading leg and a heel strike.
3. **Left and right halves don't match:** f2 has a big torso twist and skirt flare, but f6 has almost none. The arm flare happens at f3–f5 but has no match at f7–f1. Each half should mirror the other.
4. **Skirt and dress outline:** it jumps from a narrow tube (f0, f1, f7) to a wide bell (f2–f5), roughly 6 px wider each side. The spring is too loose and the flare starts all at once. Lower the stiffness so it overshoots by about 2–3 px with a 1-frame delay.
5. **Knees and hair:**
   - The knees barely bend. The passing leg in f2 and f6 needs about 3 px of knee lift with the toe pointing down. f1 and f5 need a visible knee bend of about 1–2 px to absorb the landing.
   - The bun and bow barely move. They should trail the head bob by 1 frame, about 1–2 px up and down, peaking at f1 and f5.
