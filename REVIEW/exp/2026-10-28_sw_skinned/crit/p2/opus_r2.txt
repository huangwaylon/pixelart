## Top 5 P2 defects

1. **The legs merge in the passing frames (walk f03, f04, f05, f06).** The near and far legs become one black mass from the knees to the boots, and f06 has a double-knee blob where the legs cross. The rim needs a lighter value (a full step above the stocking colour, not a dark grey) and a guaranteed 1-px gap or outline wherever the legs overlap.
2. **The skirt is a tent and its hem is jagged (walk f02–f06, run f03–f05).** The hem flares to nearly twice the master's width, with 2–3 px stair-steps at the corners and a hard flat bottom. Hem smoothing has made it stiff rather than elegant. Cap the flare and allow a gently curved hem.
3. **The skin under the hem pulses (walk f02, f03, f06, f07; idle f04–f10).** The pink patch between hem and sock band jumps between about 2 and 6 rows and changes width. Lock its height, for example to the 2–3 rows the master shows.
4. **Boot toes are lumpy (walk f04, f05, f06; run f02, f07).** The near boot turns into a square stub, a single-pixel spur sticks out under the heel in walk f05, and in walk f06 both boots fuse into one blob. Use a toe template: a 2-px taper with a dark sole row.
5. **Face and sash noise.** There are blocky pink rectangles on the face (walk f03 and f04, run f01 and f05, idle f05–f07), probably blush or shading quantised into flat blocks. The lavender sash breaks into a floating smear when the arm crosses it (idle f02–f10, run f03–f05). Pin both to master-derived masks.

**Verdict:** P2 is a clear improvement on the owner's specific complaints: legs no longer vanish and the thighs stay attached. Defects 1 and 2 should be fixed before showing it again.
