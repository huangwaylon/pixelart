Here is the art direction review. I am not going to sugarcoat this: **every single 3D-pipeline candidate is a catastrophic downgrade from the `m96 master`**, and the core problem is not the downscaler—it is the 3D model, rigging, and posing before a single pixel filter is even run.

### Part 1: Global 3D Rig & Model Failures (Applies to ALL 3D Candidates)
Before looking at individual downscalers, the 3D asset itself completely violates the illustration’s design sheet:
1. **Proportions & Silhouette Destruction:** The illustration and `m96 master` have a compact, soft, slightly chibi-adjacent silhouette with a relaxed, wide-draping kimono top. The 3D model has an elongated, hyper-pinched wasp waist, flared "tutu" skirt geometry, and gangly, overly long legs. 
2. **The Pose & Neck Seam:** The 3D rig is stuck in a stiff, awkward semi-T-pose with arms jutting out horizontally. Worse, pasting the `_hs` (head stamp) onto the 3D body creates a grotesque **giraffe-neck disconnection**. The head floats high above the collar with a thick, awkward flesh-colored cylinder bridging the gap, whereas in the `m96 master` the chin tucks naturally right into the collar/shoulders.
3. **Collar & Chest Exposure:** The illustration has a modest, closed kimono wrap. The 3D model has a wide, scooped V-neck exposing a large triangular blotch of chest skin.

### Part 3: Walk Cycle Evaluation (Image 2 vs. Image 3)

**1. Consistency & Boiling (Image 2):**
* **What works:** Because it is driven by a 3D rig, the macro-motion (leg swing arc, torso rotation) is mechanically smooth across the 8 frames.
* **What breaks (Severe Boiling):** The downscalers do not preserve sub-pixel topology frame-to-frame. 
  * **Hands & Sleeves:** Watch the extended front arm across cols 1–8—the hand flickers from 3 fingers, to a 1px dot, to completely vanishing inside the black outline. 
  * **Skirt Hem & Belt:** The 1px black outline under the skirt and around the grey belt pops in and out of existence every frame, creating heavy "pixel crawl" (outline boiling).
  * **Legs/Boots:** In cols 3, 7, and 8 (passing poses), the overlapping legs merge into a thick mass of black outline pixels, losing which leg is in front.
  * **Row 6 (`celo_kc` without head stamp):** Shows why the raw 3D head is unusable—the face boils into a terrifying, melting mask with uneven, flickering eye sockets.
  * **The `_hs` Pasted Head Problem:** In rows 1–5, pasting a 100% static 2D head onto a rotating, bobbing 3D torso makes her look like a lifeless paper cutout pinned to a moving mannequin. In cols 1, 5, and 6, the neck detaches or shifts off-center from the collar.

### Part 4: Scores & Final Verdict

* **Best Candidate Still:** **3.5 / 10** (`matv_hs` / `celo_poe_hs` — where `m96 master` = 7/10). Completely unshippable as-is due to the 3D rig's broken anatomy, giraffe neck, and T-pose arms.
* **Walk Consistency (3D Pipeline):** **4.5 / 10**. Good macro-arcs, but sub-pixel outline boiling on the sleeves, belt, and hands requires manual cleanup on every single frame anyway.
* **Walk Fidelity to Design (3D Pipeline):** **2.5 / 10**. Looks like a different character wearing a flared cheerleader skirt and a V-neck top with a dislocated neck.

#### Best Style + Downscaler Combination:
**`matv_hs` (Material Vote)** or **`cel_poe_hs` (Cel + PixelOE)**. 
* *Why:* `shade` introduces gradients that turn into dirty banding in pixel art. `celo` (3D outlines) creates double-thick, clumpy black noise when downscaled. You want **un-outlined Cel or Material IDs** downscaled cleanly (`matv` or `poe`), and then you should apply a clean 2D 1-pixel outer outline pass *after* downscaling.

#### Does Hi-Res 3D -> Pixelize Beat Pixelizing the Illustration?
**Hell no.** Pixelizing the 2D illustration + hand-polishing (`m96 master`) wins by a landslide because the 2D illustration actually has good art direction, appealing proportions, and proper clothing drape. 

**Director's Mandate to Fix the Pipeline:**
If you want to use 3D to drive animation, **stop using the 3D render as final pixels.** 
1. Fix the 3D model first: thicken the waist to match the `m96 master`, lower the head so the chin sits on the collar, close the V-neck, and make the skirt hang straight down instead of flaring out like a tutu.
2. Render the 3D walk using `matv` (Material Vote) to get clean flat color blocks, and use it **strictly as an underdrawing/rotoscope guide** (like the left side of Image 3, but swing the arms naturally along the sides instead of holding them out in a Zombie walk) to hand-pixel/clean up using the `m96 master` palette and proportions.
