## Generator-level issues

These recur across many frames and directions. Fix them procedurally instead of by hand; I deliberately did not hand-paint them.

- **G1. Temple skin pokes above or beside the hat cuff in every S frame** (for example S f12 (16,20),(16-17,21),(31,20); S f2 (16-17,28); S f3 (16,19),(29-31,19); S f7 (16-17,21-22)). The face's skin rises between the hair side flap and the end of the cuff, touching the red dome. The cuff therefore looks narrower than the head, the cuff ring is broken, and two salmon "ears" appear. Suggestion: in the decal/paint pass, any skin pixel above the fringe row that is 4-adjacent to hat, hatcuff or outline should be repainted as hair (hair_1, or hair_2 when it is in the highlight band). Alternatively, widen the rendered hatcuff band by about 1 px at its ends (scale the cuff ring radius about 1.05 so it fully covers the head mesh at the temples).
- **G2. Nape skin strip in every N frame** (the r5 art director's item 6; worst in N f2 (8px skin_1) and N f9 (2 rows); stray skin and hair pieces in N f3/f5/f10). From the back, a skin band between the bob and the collar reads as a gap, and in some frames it breaks into specks. Suggestion: lower the back of the scarf collar or raise the coat collar by about 1 px at the back. Failing that, add a post-pass for back-facing directions (N/NE/NW) that recolours skin pixels below the hair mass and above the coat to hair_1, or to plum_0 where they are next to plum. I fixed only the specks (N f3) by hand so that later generator overrides don't go stale.
- **G3. The mouth decal lands on the chin-shadow row and touches the scarf** (S f2, f3, f4, f6, f9 were fixed by hand). Suggestion: in face.py, clamp the mouth's bottom row so there is at least one skin pixel between it and any scarf/coat pixel. Shrink a 2x2 mouth to 2x1 if needed rather than letting it touch the scarf. Also anchor the mouth to the bottom of the eyes plus one row (S f12 had it level with the eyes).
- **G4. Scarf tail shadow merges with the shadow-side mitten** (S f1, f10). Both use crn_1, so when they touch they read as one blob. Suggestion: after rasterising, if a mitten pixel is 4-adjacent to a scarf pixel, force a 1px coat separator, or render the mitten with its red_1 lit pixel on the side facing the scarf.
- **G5. The light coat band (lav_1) produces 3px slivers in crouch/land poses only** (S f1, N f2, N f9). Suggestion: raise the coat light threshold, or drop lav_1 islands under about 6px in the cleanup pass (rule 2, adjusted for 48px). This removes the frame-to-frame pop.
- **G6. Boot cuffs swallow the boots in squash poses and some toe-down poses** (N f2, N f9, S f3). The cuff colour reaches the ground row. Suggestion: always reserve the bottom 1-2 opaque rows of each foot for the boot ramp (rule 8: boots darkest on the ground row), clipping the cuff band above them.
- **G7. Hands and cuffs collapse to 1px stripes when the arms are foreshortened toward or away from the camera** (N f7, N f8; S f8 had a 1px cuff spike). Suggestion: enforce a minimum 2px mitten width in the limb pass (dilate the mitten mask by 1px into the cuff/sleeve, never into the outline), and clip the cuff so it never extends past the end of the mitten.
- **Also noted, not changed (silhouette/3D):** the pom disappears for one frame in S f4 because of the head tilt-back. The N back-view hair highlight (hair_2) covers about half the hair mass in crouch/land (N f2, N f9). Consider capping hair_2 coverage per frame (for example to 25% of hair pixels) to keep it a band, in line with the art director's item 2.
