# r20 Track C, round 9: AD review 1

**Verdict:** The palette, character read and face decals are right. The construction is not there yet: helmet hair, a V-shield jaw, bow-tie bow, a tube coat with no waist and stick legs. It is not traceable as canon yet.

## Working: keep
- **Eyes.** Size (~0.22 H), low placement, thick upper lash with flick, teal iris with dark top, two catchlights, thin brows. Close to c6/c7.
- **Material colours and part separation.** Every part reads in flat colour.
- **Scarf colour, chunk and placement.** The tail is correctly on HER left (viewer's right), reaches the belt and has a fringe.
- **Coat trim language.** Rust hem band, rust cuffs, belt with gold buckle, 3+3 buttons, back centre seam.
- **Back hair.** Stays behind the arms in front and 3/4, and has a blunt V cut at the hip (base) and belt (sprite).
- **Overall sizes.** Base height ~5.4 H. Bow tip-to-tip ~1.7x head. Coat hem ~1.6 H (base) and ~1.7 H (sprite). The sprite face has room.
- **Boots.** The fur cuff and boot mass are good.

## TOP 12 fixes (ranked)

1. **Head is a helmet** (all views, worst in front and side). The hair is one shell with vertical sides and a flat-topped box silhouette. In profile the bangs stick out like a cap visor, with a hard ridge where the bangs meet the crown.
   **Fix:**
   - Round the cranium: hair top is a dome, widest at the temples (~1.15 H incl. hair), then curving in, with no vertical flats above the ears.
   - Remove the bangs/crown ridge. Let the crown sheets flow from a whorl at the back of the crown, over the top and into the bangs.
   - Profile: bangs overhang ~0.05 H past the brow, not ~0.12 H.

2. **Jaw is a V shield** (front, 3/4). The chin is long and sharp, the taper starts at the cheekbone, and there is a hard crease from ear to chin in 3/4.
   **Fix:**
   - Keep full cheeks down to ~0.7 H from the crown before the taper starts.
   - Shorten the chin by ~0.05 H and round the tip to a small soft point (radius ~0.04 H).
   - Soften the jaw edge to a smooth curve (c6).

3. **Profile face is a flat mask** (side, 3/4). The face is set back under the bangs, there is no forward cheek curve, and the nose is a spike.
   **Fix:**
   - Push the cheek forward ~0.04 H below the eye so the profile reads: brow, soft eye inset, cheek bulge, tiny nose tip, small mouth dip, small chin.
   - Inset the eye ~0.02 H behind the cheek.
   - Nose: a soft bump of ~0.02 H, not a cone. In front view it should be a tiny shadow tick (it is currently too visible).

4. **Bow loops are bow-tie triangles with flat ends** (front, back). In side view the bow is a cube/barrel.
   **Fix:**
   - Rebuild each loop as a puffy teardrop: narrow pinch at the knot, rounded bulb at the tip (bulb ~0.35 H tall), thick rounded rim, recessed hollow and one crease.
   - Angle the loops ~20° up and ~15° back.
   - Front: drop the loops ~0.05 H and tuck the roots behind the crown so they read as a bow, not handles or ears (c10).
   - Side: one teardrop seen edge-on, ~0.3 H deep, not a block.

5. **Bow tails are noodles/wires** (side, back). In side view one is a thin cable curling off the ponytail.
   **Fix:** make the tails flat ribbons ~0.08 H wide with swallowtail V ends, lying on the ponytail and ending at the shoulder blades.

6. **Coat is a tube/tent with no waist** (front, 3/4, both builds). The sides run straight from shoulder to hem, there is no bust, and the waist is invisible.
   **Fix:**
   - Pinch the bodice to 0.75 H at the belt (base) or 0.85 H (sprite), with a modest bust swell above it.
   - Flare the skirt only from the belt down.
   - Add 4-5 soft vertical skirt folds, with a slight scallop in the hem trim.

7. **Shoulders are square and the sleeves fuse into the coat** (front, back). Pose: the arms hang almost straight down.
   **Fix:**
   - Slope the shoulders, 0.9 H wide.
   - Add a slight sleeve puff at the cap (+0.04 H).
   - Rotate the arms 12-15° out with a 5-10° elbow bend, so there is a clear sky gap between sleeve and waist.

8. **Legs are uniform sticks** (base, all views). Hem to sole looks ~2.1 H of straight cylinders with no knee or calf.
   **Fix:**
   - Thighs ~0.28 H wide, tapering to the knee.
   - One soft calf curve, max ~0.24 H at upper calf.
   - Ankle ~0.14 H.
   - Close the gap between the legs slightly (feet about a fist apart).

9. **Bangs lack panels and lift** (front, 3/4). They read as one sheet with scratched lines, and the cut is thin at the outer panels.
   **Fix:**
   - Three distinct panels (centre ~40% width) with two real V-notches ~0.04 H deep.
   - Lift off the forehead ~0.03 H.
   - Uniform cut thickness ~0.025 H.
   - Slight inward curl at the tips.

10. **Hair-shine is a ring of oval beads** (all views). It reads as rivets or a headband.
    **Fix:** replace it with a broken angel-ring band. Use 5-7 tapered strokes (each ~0.12 H long, ~0.025 H thick) following the dome curvature at ~0.3 H below the crown, on the bangs and top only. Leave gaps at the panel notches (c6).

11. **Side locks are boards that float** (3/4, front). In 3/4 the near lock flares away from the cheek with a visible gap. They are tube-thick.
    **Fix:**
    - Root them in the temple under the bangs.
    - Hug the cheek and jaw (offset ≤0.02 H).
    - Thin to ~0.05 H thickness, 0.18 H wide.
    - Taper slightly to a blunt cut at the collarbone, with a slight inward curve at the bottom.

12. **Ponytail and back hair are single slabs** (side, back).
    - In side view the ponytail is a C-swoop sheet ~0.35 H thick.
    - In back view it disappears into the back hair as a dark teardrop.

    **Fix:**
    - Ponytail: 3-4 layered clumps swelling to ~0.5 H, in a true S (out and back, then in toward the spine, then a flick out) to a single point at the waist.
    - Back hair: 3-4 broad overlapping sheets with slight edge separations and a hair_shadow inner layer. The ponytail must sit proud of it (~0.08 H) so it separates in the back and 3/4 views.
