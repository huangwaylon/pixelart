# r12 M1 - HD model: engine, export, S4 hair and bow

## What was built
- **`pipeline/sdf.py`**: SDF parts.
  - **Shapes:** ellipsoid, rbox, capsule, rcone, cone (flat-cut), tube (polyline), and torus (elliptical band ring with taper).
  - **Flattening:** per-point flattening, interpolated along each segment.
  - **Combination:** smooth-min, subtract and intersect act only inside a `blend.set`. A hard min applies everywhere else.
  - **Clips:** legacy clip semantics.
  - **Tracing:** per-part bounds culled through a 2D ray grid, then at most 48 float64 sphere-tracing steps.
  - **Output:** returns the same `(t, prim, normal)` buffers as the legacy raycaster. `pose_parts` handles bones, weighted `bones`, LOD (`all` / `ref`) and chain sweeps.
- **Render path:**
  - `"model": "hd"` selects `Scene.tracer`; `render_pixels` and `render_smooth` take `tracer=`.
  - chargen emits `parts` (legacy ellipsoids converted 1:1, plus `parts`, `remove_parts`, `part_overrides` and `hd_chains`).
  - The legacy `primitives` remain the collision proxies for the sim and QA.
- **`pipeline/meshexport.py`**: numpy marching tetrahedra, one shell per material. It writes a binary STL and an OBJ/MTL for the rest pose and the jump apex (about 90k triangles, 4.5 MB).
- **`data/variants/S4_hd.json`**: generated by `pipeline/tools/make_s4_hd.py`. 56 part specs expand to about 300 posed primitives.

## Gates (jump_sc)
| Gate | Result |

| Legacy-only parts through the HD path | Frame-identical (IoU 1.0) |

| `--verify` | IDENTICAL |
| Build time | 39.1 s vs 20.1 s legacy (1.95x), including about 10 s of mesh export |

## Known gaps
- The back curtain is head-rigid, not 5 chains.
- Faint rings at sharp tube bends in the hi-res refs.
- STL hair is softer than the refs (0.3-0.4 grid); material shells interpenetrate.
- Not yet: `detail` modifiers, idle-pose STL, QA rule for sub-pixel `lod: all` parts.
