# r13 Design Bible: the heroine

Blocking: `art/hand/S4/design/blocking_front.pix` -> `00_REVIEW/r13_anime_pixel/design/blocking_front_x4.png`

## Identity
- **Name:** Koyuki ("little snow"), called Yuki.
- **Age feel:** 19-20. Cute, not childlike.
- **Personality in the body:** cheerful = chest up; plucky = weight forward; clumsy = toes in; shy = shoulders up, knees in.

## Proportions (H = crown to chin)
- **Height:** 5.4 H. In the blocking H = 22 px (y5-y27).
- **Widths:** shoulders 0.9 H (sloped, narrower than the hair), waist 0.75 H (belt at 2.05 H), hips 1.0 H, hem 1.6 H.
- **Legs:** 2.4 H, knee at the midpoint, one calf bulge, slim ankles.
- **Curves:** 1 px bust step, waist pinch, hem flare.
- **Game sprite (3.4 H):** same head shapes, bow ratio and colours; torso and legs squashed (legs 1.1 H).

## Face
- **Shape:** soft oval, 18 px wide, full cheek. The jaw tapers over 6 rows to a 4 px chin.
- **Eyes:** rounded-top **tare-me**, 5x6 px, one eye-width apart, set low in the face. Tare-me reads warm; pluck comes from the brows and a 1-2 px lash flick.
- **Lashes:** thick ink upper line, 1 px past the outer corner; 1 px lower lash.
- **Iris:** iris_0 top, iris_1 body, iris_2 glint, 2 px snow_3 catchlight (same side always).
- **Brows:** 3 px hair_1 strokes just under the bang line.
- **Mouth:** 2 px crn_1. **Signature:** red_2 blush dashes.

## Hair (shapes)
- **Hime bangs:** blunt cut 1 px above the brows. 2 notches = 3 panels, centre widest.
- **Side locks:** 4 px wide, temple to collarbone, blunt cut.
- **Ponytail:** gathered at the back of the crown (brow height in side view), under the bow knot. Swells to 0.5 H, tapers in a slight S to a sharp point at the waist.
- **Back hair:** long and straight, blunt slight-V cut at the hip.
- **Bow (BIG):** about 2x head width, wings past the head in front view; loops pinched at the knot, one crease each; swallowtail tails to the shoulder blades.
- **Silhouette:** front = wings, locks, flare; 3/4 = wing plus ponytail point; side = ponytail arc; back = bow, tails, point.

## Outfit
- **Coat:** mid-thigh A-line, fitted to the waist; collar hidden by the scarf.
- **Details:** 6 centre buttons (3 above the belt), 3 px belt, 3 px rust hem and cuff trim.
- **Scarf:** chunky double wrap, 0.3 H thick. ONE tail on **HER LEFT** (the viewer's right in front view), to the belt, teal_2 fringe; fixed in every view.
- **Rest:** rounded mittens, dark tights, mid-calf boots with cream cuffs.

## Expressions (eyes / brows / mouth / extra)
1. **neutral:** open / flat / 2 px line / none.
2. **smile:** lower lid up 1 px / raised / small "u" / light blush.
3. **laugh:** closed "^^" / high arch / open "D" with crn_2 tongue / blush.
4. **surprised:** wide, 3 px irises / floating high / small "o" / sweat ticks.
5. **sad:** half-lid, looking down / inner ends up / downturned / outer tear.
6. **angry:** flat-cut top lid / inner ends sharply down / "^" pout, puffed cheek / vein mark.
7. **embarrassed:** averted / wavy / "~" / 3-row nose blush, steam.
8. **determined:** flattened top lid / straight, down-in / firm, pulled to one side / catchlight sparkle.
9. **worried:** looking sideways / inner up, wavy / small flat / one sweat drop.
10. **content:** closed "u" arcs / relaxed low / closed smile / light blush.

- **Distinctness:** laugh vs content = open vs closed mouth; sad vs worried = down + tear vs sideways + sweat; angry vs determined = pout vs side-pull.

## Poses (line of action / weight / hands)
- **idle:** soft C, weight on right leg / curled mittens.
- **wave:** S-curve up the raised right arm / open palm.
- **point:** heel-to-fingertip diagonal, leaning in / index out.
- **hands_on_hips:** vertical, chest out / fists on belt, elbows wide.
- **recoil:** reverse C, one foot up, ponytail flicks / open hands at chest.
- **shy:** inward curl, knees in / mittens clasped under chin.
- **shiver:** compact vertical, 1 px jitter / hugging herself.
- **cheer:** Y-line, hop, tails lift / both fists up.
