# r21 round 1: self-critique of S4_hd2 against the canon (c1-c10, design bible)

Ranked by how much each issue hurts, worst first.

1. **Ponytail reads as a carrot.** In the back view it is wide straight from the knot and its 3 clumps merge into one cone. Canon (c5, c9): narrow at the gather, swelling to about 0.5 H, then an S taper to a point. The S curve shows in the side view, but faint joint bands run across it (n=30 samples is too coarse for its length).
2. **Scarf tail too thin.** The ribbon tail (w 2.5, t 0.8) reads as a strap. In the S sprite it nearly vanishes next to the old chunky tail. Canon: chunky knit, same thickness as the wrap.
3. **Fringe root.** The cap's front cut leaves a lip above the panels, with small pinch creases where the 3 panels meet it. The panels read as 3 boards from the side (stepped edges).
4. **Temple wisps (lod ref)** now poke through the cheeks as dark slivers in 3/4 and side views (the old fat side clumps used to hide them).
5. **Bow in E/W sprites** is a 4x7 teal block (the near loop seen through its opening). In the hi-res views the front and back read well: wide horizontal wings, dark hollow, thick rim, swept back. The S sprite still shows the outer halves as small rings at the crown corners.
6. **Back curtain sheets** step like plates at the sides in the 3/4 and side views. The layering offset (dz 0.2) shows as separate boards.
7. **Head close-up framing** crops the crown, and the knot is barely visible (tool issue).
8. **Coat:** the soft folds are very subtle at sprite scale, which is fine. The sleeve puff barely shows.

What already works vs S4_hd:
- **Crown:** one clean dome with flow grooves, no blob rows.
- **Fringe:** 3 blunt panels with 2 notches instead of the 7-point zigzag.
- **Side locks:** straight, flat and blunt.
- **Bow:** horizontal satin wings in the N sprite, which match c9.
- **Mittens:** about 1.55x volume.
- **QA:** idle and walk QA PASS, heights equal to S4_hd (34/35 px).
