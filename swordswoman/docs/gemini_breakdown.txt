### II. Inconsistencies & Canonical Recommendations

As Art Director, here is how we resolve the discrepancies between the hero render (Img 1) and the action sketches (Img 2 & 3) for the final 3D model/production bible:

1.  **Footwear (The biggest discrepancy):** 
    *   *Issue:* Image 1 shows high heels. Images 2 and 3 show flat, ballet-style pointed shoes.
    *   *Ruling:* **Treat Image 1 (Heels) as Canonical.** The heels contribute significantly to the character's stylized, elegant silhouette. The sketches likely used flats simply because they are easier to draw quickly in dynamic poses.
2.  **Corset Detail:**
    *   *Issue:* Image 1 has intricate straps and cutouts. Images 2/3 show a solid dark mass.
    *   *Ruling:* **Treat Image 1 as Canonical.** The sketches are just shorthand. The modelers must build the complex strapped version.
3.  **Sword Design:**
    *   *Issue:* Image 1 has a specific, horned tsuba (guard) and wrapped handle. Images 2/3 show a generic stick/basic katana.
    *   *Ruling:* **Treat Image 1 as Canonical.** The prop team must build the stylized sword.
4.  **Right Shoulder Ornament:**
    *   *Issue:* Present in Image 1, missing in 2 and 3.
    *   *Ruling:* **Keep it.** It breaks up the symmetry of the red top nicely.

### III. 64px Chibi Pixel Art Translation Guide

When downscaling this design to a 64-pixel tall chibi sprite, readability is paramount. We must rely on the "squint test." Here is what stays and what goes:

**What SURVIVES (Exaggerate these):**
*   **The Silhouette:** The massive red hair bow (make it huge, maybe 1/4 the size of the head).
*   **Color Blocking:** The stark contrast between the pale skin, the bright red dress, and the dark brown/black corset block.
*   **The Collar:** A few pixels of red framing the lower face.
*   **The Skirt Shape:** The high-low cut. Show bare legs in the front, with a block of red pixels trailing behind.
*   **The Sword:** A simple silver line with a dark handle. The unique guard can be represented by a single silver pixel jutting out.
*   **Eyes:** Bright blue pixels to contrast the red.

**What MUST BE DROPPED (Too noisy for 64px):**
*   **Corset Straps:** Drop all the individual horizontal straps and cutouts. The corset becomes a solid dark brown rectangle/hourglass shape. You might keep *one* lighter brown pixel to imply a buckle.
*   **Shoe Laces/Straps:** Drop the crisscross ankle straps entirely. The feet just become dark grey/black pointed pixels (heels will be hard to read at this scale, just make them dark boots/shoes).
*   **Shoulder Ornament:** Drop the purple knot. It will look like a glitch or a mistake at 64px.
*   **Sword Handle Wrap:** Drop the red diamonds. The handle is just a solid dark grey line.
*   **Thigh Strap:** Highly likely to be dropped, or reduced to a single dark pixel line if leg real estate allows.
*   **Scarf Ribbons:** Unless the character is in an idle animation where they are floating, drop the extra pink ribbons to avoid muddying the silhouette of the main dress.
