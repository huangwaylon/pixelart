96 PX FIGURE RULES (128x128 cell, figure ~96 px crown->sole; palette = the legend; copy the master's treatment of every material):
- PROPORTIONS: keep the master's proportions exactly (head/torso/skirt/leg lengths, sleeve width, obi height, sock band + thigh skin band + boots). The guide gives POSE and foreshortening only; where guide and master disagree on size or thickness, the MASTER wins.
- OUTLINE: every silhouette edge pixel is K (1 px; 2 px only at boot soles and under the hair mass). Inner lines are the material's darkest ramp char, not K: dress/sleeve folds and seams = R, hair strands = h, boots = h, skin creases = S/m, obi edges = o. Lines stepping 1 px per row use equal step lengths (no 1-3-1).
- SHADING: light from the upper left. Each volume = base + 1 shadow tone on its right third and under overhangs (under the obi R, under the sleeves R, under the hem on the thighs S) + light tone sparingly on upper-left edges (e on the sleeve tops/shoulder, Q on the obi top edge, d on the socks, g/L glints on the boots). Clusters >= 2 px, no orphan pixels, no pillow shading (never ring a shape with its shadow).
- HAIR BELOW THE HEAD: the long hair ends hanging below the locked stamp continue the stamp's strands (H base, h strands/shadow, L sheen only if the master has it there), taper to points, and swing 1-2 px with the motion (secondary motion, 1-frame lag).
- LEGS: below the hem: skin band (s/S), then the black sock band (D with d light, K outline), then boots (H base, h shadow, g/L toe glint). Far leg 1 tone darker where overlapped.
- MOTION: follow the guide pose (limb angles, step length, foot placement, body lean). Standing feet stand exactly on the pivot row. Moving parts may be cheated across the screen where the 3D depth foreshortens them, but static parts (anything the guide does not move between neighbouring frames) must stay pixel-identical to the previous frame - no boiling.
- 1x READABILITY: clear silhouette (arms separate from the body where the guide separates them), no noise, no single-pixel speckle; the figure must read at 1x.
- METHOD: start from the master's body pixels (same materials, shading clusters, fold lines, hair-end shapes) and re-pose them to the guide, rather than inventing new rendering. The master PNG v2/m96/masters/EC0_<VIEW>_96.png shows it at 1x.
