# Art director review: A (frontE_4) vs B (frontE_1)

**Overall:** Both look clean and appealing, but neither yet has the poise of Lyra or Dawn. Both read as a chibi-leaning anime front view rather than a Sugimori trainer. The top half is heavy: the bob plus the bow is about 1.9× shoulder width, so the head dominates.

**Estimated heads tall** (skull only, bun and bow left out):
- **A:** about 3.4. Legs are about 41% of height.
- **B:** about 3.3. The head is slightly larger, the sleeves wider and the arms splay out, so it reads a bit stockier.

**Anything fat, stubby or awkward?**
- **Boots:** stubby and blocky in both, like clogs. This is the worst problem.
- **Obi:** a flat rectangle, so the waist reads boxy rather than about 0.5 of head width.
- **Neck:** present but short.
- **Shoulders:** about 0.66 of head width, which is OK.

**Is the face Sugimori-like?** Partly. The eyes sit low enough, but they're generic wide almonds with big irises. Sugimori eyes are taller and narrower, with a heavy upper lash line and a smaller iris. B's smile is too wide.

**Scores:** A 7/10, B 6/10. **Pick A.**

**Top 4 fixes for A:**
1. **Boots:** make them taller (mid-calf), slimmer, and tapered at the ankle with a small heel. That lengthens the line of the legs.
2. **Head mass:** shrink the hair volume about 10%, pull the bob in toward the jaw and make the bow slightly smaller. That brings it to about 3.4–3.5 heads.
3. **Waist:** curve the obi and pinch it in to about 0.5 of head width. Flare the hem slightly and narrow the sleeves so the silhouette tapers.
4. **Face:** make the eyes taller and narrower with a thick upper lash and a smaller iris, use a tiny nose and mouth, and lengthen the neck about 15%. Also give it a contrapposto stance so it doesn't stand flat in an A-pose.
