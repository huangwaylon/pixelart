## Round 2 review

### 1. Scores

| | Score | One-line verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Original concept | 5 | Charming, but there's too much in it for 48 px (corset buckles, ribbons, sandal straps, scarf) |

### 2. A vs B, and which weapon option is primary

- **Go with A. Kill the collar.** In the probe, B loses the skin break at the neck and shoulders, and that break is the only thing separating head from body at 40–48 px. In 3D, a funnel collar clips the chin and hair on every head turn and look-down attack.
- **Primary reference: A_N** for 3D reconstruction and the rig. Make the katana a separate prop sheet (side view plus top view, hilt detail).
- **Keep A_K** as the secondary in-game identity and pose reference.
- **Drop A_S completely.** A hip saya collides with the sleeve and skirt in every run frame, and it reads as noise.

### 3. Remaining problems and fixes

1. **The sleeves merge into the skirt.** The bell sleeves hang to the skirt hem line, so the arms disappear into the skirt silhouette at 48 px. This is the biggest readability problem.
   - Shorten the sleeves so they end at the bottom edge of the obi, about 1/3 shorter.
   - Reduce the flare to about 1.4× the arm width.
   - Show a dark lining (#7A1E24) in the bell opening so the sleeve shape reads as its own shape.
2. **Same red everywhere.** Bow, torso, sleeves and skirt are all one value (~#B8323A).
   - Torso and skirt: #B8323A.
   - Sleeves one step darker: #9E2A31.
   - Bow one step brighter and warmer: #D23A3A.
   - This gives three readable red zones instead of one.
3. **The skirt tails are jagged and too long.** The asymmetric pointed tails, especially the back tail reaching the ankle, turn into spiky pixel noise in the probe. They would also need two or three secondary bones each.
   - Cut the hem to a simple trapezoid ending just above the knee, with the front slit only.
   - At most, keep one short rounded back flap ending at mid-calf.
4. **The legs are too short for lively animation.** At 48 px the legs are about 6 px, which isn't enough for a run cycle with readable contact poses.
   - Make the legs about 20% longer, taking the height from the skirt rather than the head.
   - Target 8–9 px from hip to sole.
5. **The hands are buried.** The sword hand is hidden inside the sleeve, so the grip doesn't read and swing arcs will look like they come from nowhere.
   - Show a 2×2 px fist below the sleeve opening with a dark fingerless glove (#2A2026). The glove gives good contrast against both the red and the steel.
6. **The katana breaks up.** In the probe the blade becomes a dotted 1 px line, and the tsuba is gone.
   - In pixel art, draw the blade 2 px wide (#C9D3DC with a #8C98A6 edge) at a clean 2:1 angle.
   - Make the tsuba 2 px gold (#B08A3A) so the hand and hilt read.
   - Length is fine at about 0.6× the character's height.
7. **The detached sleeves are inconsistent.** A_S and A_N have the dark arm band at the top of the sleeve; A_K doesn't. Keep the band, 2 px, same colour as the obi. It separates shoulder skin from sleeve and gives a clean seam in 3D.
8. **Palette drift.** Hair goes from warm brown (A_K, A_N) to near-black (B_K), and B_K adds a purple skirt lining. Lock these values:
   - Hair: #3A2C2E.
   - Obi: #4E2E2A.
   - Shorts and boots: #26201F.
   - Skin: #F4D6BE, shade #E0B49A.
   - Eye: #4F7FA8.
9. **Interior lines.** The skirt overlap, lapels and sleeve folds are still drawn with interior lines, which breaks the outline-only-outside rule. They vanish at 48 px anyway. Replace them with a one-step value change.

### 4. Simple enough?

Almost. Do one more pass and change only these things:

- Shorten the sleeves (#1).
- Replace the skirt tails with one simple hem (#3).
- Remove the hair highlight band; keep one flat value plus one shade.
- Make the boots and shorts one colour (#26201F) so the legs read as dark–skin–dark.
- Remove all interior lines.
- Bun and bow: make the bow about 15% bigger and the bun smaller, mostly hidden behind it. The bow is your strongest identifier in every probe size, so it should own the top of the silhouette.

Don't simplify the face, the V-neck skin triangle, the bare shoulders or the obi. Those are what carry the read.

### 5. Problems for 3D reconstruction

- **Sleeves fused with the skirt** in the 3/4 view: TRELLIS/Pixal3D will weld them into one mesh. Give the reference a 25–30° A-pose with a visible gap between sleeve and skirt.
- **Painted hair highlight band:** it gets baked into the texture and looks wrong under rotation. Remove it from the reference; do highlights in the shader or by hand on the sprite.
- **Back of the head is undefined:** the bun's position, how the bob is cut at the nape, and whether the bow knot has tails. You need a back view, especially since 4-view input won in the earlier test.
- **Wrap direction:** check that the lapels and skirt are left-over-right (migi-mae: the wearer's left panel on top). Right-over-left is the burial wrap. Keep it consistent across all views.
- **Long skirt tails and bell sleeves** become extra cloth bones or physics. The cuts in #1 and #3 remove most of that cost.
- **Skirt slit and shorts:** in the reference, the slit has to clearly show the shorts as a separate layer. Otherwise reconstruction fills the slit in or merges the shorts into the legs.
