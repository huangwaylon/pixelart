**(2) Intentional / Pokemon-like:**
No. Official BW/HGSS trainers (Lyra, Hilda, Rosa) use **2x3 or 2x4 vertical eyes** with a 1px white highlight and 1-2px unbordered mouths. Your eyes are horizontal/squat (3x2), and your open mouths have heavy dark borders around pink/white fills, creating a Western-indie look rather than Game Freak’s clean anime shorthand.
