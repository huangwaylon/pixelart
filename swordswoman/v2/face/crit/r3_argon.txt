* **Determined (7.5):** Flat horizontal lids read sleepy/bored rather than fierce. Needs diagonal inner brow drop.
* **Shout (6.5):** S/SE mouth has a dark outline completely encircling pink—reads as thick lipstick or a stuck-out tongue at 1x.
* **Effort (6):** 3x2 white mouth block with dark border looks like buck teeth or foaming, not a grit grimace.
* **Joy (9):** Very clean `^ ^` eyes and blush. Official quality.

* **Pain (8):** `> <` reads clearly, though S/SE mouth is slightly noisy.

**(2) Pokemon Grammar:** 8/10. Eye glints and sclera-to-iris ratios match Gen 5 well. However, dark-outlined mouths (*Shout*, *Effort*, *Smile* S) violate BW trainer style, which uses 1–2px dark/red clusters without full black borders at this scale (see Lyra/Dawn refs).

**(3) Personality:** 8/10. *SE Neutral*, *SE Smile*, and *Joy* nail the spirited swordswoman vibe. *Determined* and *Effort* need sharper aggression.

**(4) Views:**
* **S (7/10):** Symmetrical mouths (*Shout*, *Effort*, *Smile*) look boxy/awkward.
* **SE (8.5/10):** Strongest row; great 3/4 appeal (even better on C_r4 test body).
* **E (7.5/10):** Forward eye placement works, but *Shout*/*Smile* mouths sit 1px too far back from the chin tip instead of breaking/touching the profile edge.
* **NE/N (8.5/10):** Good silhouette. NE lacks a 1px cheek/lash sliver to distinguish it clearly from N at 1x.

**(5) Top 6 Pixel Edits:**
1. **Shout mouth (S/SE):** Remove bottom/side dark outline pixels; make it a 3x2 dark-red top row + pink bottom row (classic `D` shape).
2. **Determined vs Blink-Half:** In *Determined*, drop the inner corner of the dark upper lid down 1px (replacing top-inner sclera/iris pixel) for an angry `\` `/` slant.
3. **Effort mouth (S/SE):** Replace the white/black box with a 3x1 horizontal white bar (teeth) capped only by 1px dark corners, or a jagged 3x1 dark grimace.
4. **Smile mouth (S):** Replace the 3-wide dark U with a 2-wide dark line + 1px raised corner (matching SE's smirk).
5. **Surprised eyes:** Delete the bottom lash pixel; expose 1px of white sclera *below* the blue iris to sell shock.
6. **E-view mouths (Shout/Effort/Pain):** Shift mouth pixels 1px right to indented/touch the front facial outline.
