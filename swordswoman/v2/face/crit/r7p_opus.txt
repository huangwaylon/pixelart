# PMD portrait review: v5 swordswoman vs. Meloetta

## Criteria (official PMD = 9)
- **Readable at 1x: 6.** The eyes hold up at 1x. In about half the set the mouth disappears.
- **Intentionally designed: 5.** The eye shapes are mostly good. Mouths look like placeholders and the brows barely change between emotions.
- **Pokemon/PMD-like: 5.** The face is cropped too tight, the head is always front-on and the same size, and there's no shoulder or gesture. Meloetta's head tilts, leans and gets hands into the frame.
- **Personality: 5.** The emotions are generic. Nothing reads as a cocky swordswoman: no smirk, no lopsided grin.

## Per emotion
- **Neutral 6:** The mouth is a 2px blush-coloured dash. It needs a dark 3–4px line.
- **Determined 5:** The brows are angled but the eyes are unchanged and the mouth is still a dot. It needs narrowed lids and a set or smirking mouth.
- **Shout 6:** The squint and open mouth read. The mouth isn't big enough; Meloetta's takes up about a third of the face.
- **Effort/Angry 4:** The gritted teeth come out as a striped box and the eyes are neutral. It needs a heavy V-brow cutting into the top of the eye.
- **Joy 7:** Strongest of the set. ^^ eyes, open mouth, blush.
- **Smile 5:** Almost the same as Joy. Use open eyes with a soft closed smile instead.
- **Surprised 6:** The "o" mouth works. The eyes need to grow taller with smaller pupils.
- **Pain 6:** The >< eyes and sweat drop read, but the mouth is noise at 1x.
- **Worried 5:** Reads as sleepy. The brows need to tip up much more steeply and the mouth needs a wobble.
- **Inspired 7:** The sparkle pupils are good. It needs a wider grin.
- **Teary 7:** The tears and wavy mouth read clearly.
- **Blink/Stunned 4:** Blank white eyes with no shock in them. Add a sweat drop, shrink the irises to dots and drop the jaw.

## Top 5 pixel fixes
1. **Mouths:** Draw every mouth in the dark outline colour, at least 4px wide, with a filled dark interior and a 1px tongue or teeth accent.
2. **Brows:** Make them 2px thick and 5px long, with a different angle per emotion, and let them overlap the eye's top line.
3. **Eyes:** Vary the eye shape per emotion: narrowed lid for Determined, taller eyes for Surprised, shrunken irises for Stunned.
4. **Framing:** Pull back about 4px so the hair crown and shoulders show, and tilt or shift the head on high-energy emotions.
5. **Shading and Smile:** Add a one-step cheek and jaw shadow. Separate Smile from Joy.
