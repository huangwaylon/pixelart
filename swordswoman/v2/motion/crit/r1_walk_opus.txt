The new version has a clearer stride than the old one, especially in the E view, and the 8-frame timing is right. Three things hold it back.

**Top 3 fixes:**

1. **Body bob.** Contact (+3.5 px) and down (+3.6 px) sit almost level, and so do pass (+0.4) and up (−0.3). The body jumps between two heights instead of easing. Raise contact/contact2 by about 1.5 px so it sits halfway, push down/down2 1 px lower to make it the lowest frame, and keep up/up2 the highest.

3. **Hair and arms barely move.** The side locks, back hair and bow move under 1.2 px, which won't show at 1x. Raise the spring gain about 2.5× so they lag 2–3 px behind the down and pass frames.
