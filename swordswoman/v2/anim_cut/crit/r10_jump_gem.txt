### 3 Most Important Concrete Problems

**1. Broken Lower-Body Anatomy and Skirt Clipping in Squash/Crouch Frames**
*   **Frames & Regions:** Frame 0 (`antic`), Frame 1 (`crouch`), Frame 7 (`land`), and Frame 8 (`recover`) — specifically the lower torso, skirt hem, and legs.
*   **The Problem:** In the standing frames (9, 10), the legs emerge cleanly from the center of the skirt. In the squash/crouch frames (0, 1, 7, 8), the leg sprites are pushed outward to the extreme left and right edges of the widened skirt to create a "frog-legged" silhouette. In Frames 0 and 8, the legs look completely detached from a pelvis, bowing outward in jagged 1-pixel curves. Furthermore, in Frames 1 and 7, the torso is squashed by simply sliding the head down over the chest, completely deleting the V-neck collar and sash, turning the dress into an unreadable flat red rectangle with a horizontal black line through it.
*   **Exact Fix:** Bring the legs inward by 3–4 pixels on each side in Frames 0, 1, 7, and 8 so they remain anchored under the hips. Instead of bowing the legs outward, push the knees *forward* and overlap the skirt hem over the upper thighs. In Frames 1 and 7, redraw the torso rather than vertically scaling/clipping it: keep 2 pixels of the white/skin V-neck visible beneath the chin so the chest doesn't vanish into the belt.

**2. Severe Cutout-Rotation "Jaggies" and Broken Outlines on the Katana**
*   **Frames & Regions:** All frames, most severely in Frame 1 (`crouch`), Frame 3 (`rise`), Frame 5 (`hang`), and Frame 7 (`land`) — the katana blade and hilt down to the bottom-left of the character.
*   **The Problem:** The katana is clearly a single sprite being rotated programmatically without hand-cleanup. This causes massive pixel-art violations: the black outline breaks into stair-stepping doubles (thick L-shapes) and intermittent gaps, and the white/grey blade core varies wildly in thickness (from 1px in Frame 2 to 3px chunkiness in Frame 3). In Frame 6 (`fall`), where the blade is horizontal, the top outline is a straight black line, while the bottom outline has a random downward jagged step near the hilt.
*   **Exact Fix:** Never use raw nearest-neighbor rotation on thin geometric sprites like swords. Redraw the katana by hand at fixed pixel-art angles: strict 45-degree (1:1 pixel steps) for Frames 0, 2, 4, 8; strict 26.5-degree (2:1 pixel steps) for Frames 1, 5, 7, 9, 10; and strict 63.5-degree (1:2 pixel steps) for Frame 3. Ensure a uniform 1px dark outline and a clean 1–2px blade width across every single frame.
