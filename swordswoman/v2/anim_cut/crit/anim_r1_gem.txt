Here is a strict pixel-art direction pass. While the silhouette and colors are charming, this suffers heavily from typical "cutout-rig laziness": parts translate by 1px vertically without secondary deformation, rotated sprites exhibit severe jagged pixel aliasing (especially the katana and legs), and high-energy actions (slash, dash, jump) lack genuine bodily squash-and-stretch or silhouette distortion.

### 1. IDLE
**Score: 5.5 / 10**
*Reads as a rigid elevator ride rather than organic breathing. The entire head/torso block shifts down as a static stamp.*

1. **Zero Overlapping Action on Hair Bow (Frames 1 & 3, top head region `y: 4–14`):** 
   * **Problem:** The bow moves down on Frame 1 (`sink`) and up on Frame 3 (`rise`) locked 1:1 with the skull. 
   * **Fix:** Offset the bow by **+1px up** on Frame 1 (lagging behind the sinking head), flatten its loops by **1px vertically** on Frame 2 (`low`) as it settles, and squash/drag the bow tips **-1px down** on Frame 3 (`rise`) as the head pushes upward.
2. **Identical In-Between Silhouettes (Frames 1 & 3, full torso/head):** 
   * **Problem:** Frame 1 (`160ms sink`) and Frame 3 (`160ms rise`) are nearly pixel-identical. This creates mechanical ping-ponging.
   * **Fix:** On Frame 3 (`rise`), lead with the chest: shift the collar/V-neck **+1px up** ahead of the sleeves, and keep the sleeves/hands **-1px lower** than Frame 1 to show gravity drag.
3. **Static Skirt Hem & Sleeves (Frame 2 `low`, `y: 42–48`):** 
   * **Problem:** At the lowest point of the breath, the kimono hem and wide sleeves stay rigid instead of flaring out to show weight.
   * **Fix:** Widen the bottom skirt hem and outer sleeve edges by **+1px outward** (left and right) on Frame 2 (`320ms low`) to create a subtle squash.

### 2. WALK
**Score: 5.0 / 10**
*The katana acts like a glued walking stick, and the front/back leg depth sorting fails to read clearly between cycles.*

1. **Frozen Katana Angle Across All 8 Frames (Frames 0–7, bottom-right quadrant):** 
   * **Problem:** The sword stays locked at the exact same ~65° angle throughout the entire walk cycle, merely translating up and down with the torso. It looks weightless and robotic.
   * **Fix:** Rotate the katana tip **+3px to +4px forward/up** on `up` frames (Frames 3 & 7) and let it dip **-2px downward** with a slight lag on `down` frames (Frames 1 & 5).
2. **Broken Passing Pose Leg Readability (Frame 2 `pass` vs. Frame 6 `pass2`, `y: 46–58`):**

3. **Weak Up/Down Bounce Amplitude (Frames 1 & 5 `down` vs. Frames 3 & 7 `up`):** 
   * **Problem:** The vertical head/torso delta between `down` and `up` is barely 2px, making the walk feel stiff rather than bouncy.
   * **Fix:** Drop the head and torso an extra **-1px down** on Frames 1 & 5 (widening the sleeves by 1px), and reduce `down` timing from `100ms` to **`80ms`** while holding `up` (Frames 3 & 7) at **`120ms`** for a snappier "pop" at the top of the step.

### 6. SLASH
**Score: 4.0 / 10**
*For the hero combat action, this lacks all impact: the body barely moves between Smear, Impact, and Hitstop, and the smear arc looks like a thin, jagged MS Paint curve rather than a sword swing.*

1. **Thin, Jagged, Disconnected Smear Arc (Frame 2 `SMEAR` & Frame 3 `impact`, right side):** 
   * **Problem:** The slash smear on Frame 2 is a 2px–3px wide yellow/white ribbon that curves vertically like a parenthesis `)` completely detached from the sword hilt, plus a weird stray 1px yellow line floating behind her head (`x: 16, y: 24`). It does not connect the `coil` blade angle to the `impact` blade angle.
   * **Fix:** Redraw the smear on Frame 2 as a **wide, tapering crescent wedge**: **10px–12px thick** near the outer bottom-right impact edge, tapering cleanly to a **1px point** up at the top-left (`coil` origin), overlapping and hiding the blade itself on Frame 2. Remove the fading yellow arc on Frame 3 (`impact`)—by impact, the smear should vanish or dissolve into 2–3 detached trailing pixels.
2. **Zero Body Lunge or Torso Rotation (Frames 2, 3, 4 `SMEAR` / `impact` / `hitstop`):** 
   * **Problem:** Her head, torso, and legs stay completely static in the exact same screen coordinates across Frames 2, 3, and 4. There is no weight behind the swing, and no hit-stop vibration.
   * **Fix:** On Frame 2 (`SMEAR`), lunge the entire body **+4px right** and drop the hips **-2px down** into a wide combat stance. On Frame 3 (`impact`), push **+1px further right** with the head/chest leaning forward. On Frame 4 (`hitstop`), shift the character **-1px left** (recoil shake) while keeping the blade tip locked on the impact spark.
3. **Sword Tip Never Reaches the Ground/Target Plane (Frames 2–4, `y: 40–56`):** 
   * **Problem:** At `impact` (Frame 3), the sword is held at the exact same casual downward-right angle as her Idle/Walk pose! A heavy overhead slash should follow through low or wide, not stop at her default resting sword angle.
