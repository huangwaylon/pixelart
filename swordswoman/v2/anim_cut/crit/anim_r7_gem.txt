Here is the director's critique for Round 7. While timing and FX have seen surface improvements, the animation still suffers heavily from "rigid cutout-rig syndrome"—parts are translating without volume deformation, overlapping secondary action on the hair/bow is minimal or mistimed, and the slash smear is severely underdeveloped.

### IDLE
1. **Frames 1–3 (`sink`, `low`, `rise`) — Rigid Head/Bow Translation (No Lag):** As the torso drops by 2px into `low` (f2), the top ribbon bow and lower hair tips drop in simultaneous 1:1 lockstep with the skull. **Fix:** In f1 (`sink`), offset the bow loops +1px up and flare the outer hair tips 1px outward to show drag. In f2 (`low`), drop the bow loops 1px lower than the resting head offset (squash), and in f3 (`rise`), delay the bow recovery by keeping it -1px lower while the head rises.
2. **Frames 0–10 — Dead Katana Welded to Hip:** The katana blade angle (~35°) and pixel cluster remain completely frozen relative to the pelvis across the entire breathing cycle, moving up and down like a stiff cardboard prop. **Fix:** In f2 (`low`) and f7 (`low-blink2`), pivot the sword tip -2px downward (flattening the angle slightly as the hand presses down) and restore it in `rise`/`top`.
3. **Frames 6, 7, 8 (`sink-half2`, `low-blink2`, `low-half2`) — Linear/Robotic Blink:** Going from half-lid (120ms) -> closed (100ms) -> half-lid (100ms) totaling 320ms is way too slow and symmetrical, reading like a sleepy animatronic rather than a snappy pixel blink. **Fix:** Cut f6 entirely (go straight from open eyes in f5 to closed in f7, or reduce f6 to 40ms), hold f7 (`low-blink2`) for 70ms, and keep f8 (`low-half2`) at 60ms for a fast snap-shut, slow-open rhythm.
