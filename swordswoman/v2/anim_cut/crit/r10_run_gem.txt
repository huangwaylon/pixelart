### 3 Most Important Concrete Problems

1. **Rotational Jaggies on Katana (Frames 2, 5, 6 - Lower Left Region)**
   * **Problem:** Automated sprite rotation creates staircase artifacts, broken outlines, and inconsistent blade thickness.
   * **Fix:** Hand-pixel the blade at fixed 1-to-1 or 2-to-1 pixel slopes to maintain a uniform 1px dark outline and solid white/grey core.

2. **Rigid Head/Hair and Missing Overlap (Frames 0–7 - Upper Body Region)**
   * **Problem:** The head, hair, and bow translate vertically as a static block without secondary motion, making the run feel stiff.
   * **Fix:** Offset the bow and lower hair tips by 1–2 pixels vertically: squash the bow down on `down` frames (1, 5) and drag the hair tips down on `push/air` frames (2, 3, 6, 7).

3. **Leg Depth Ambiguity (Frames 0 vs 4, 2 vs 6 - Lower Body Region)**
   * **Problem:** Both halves of the cycle use identical brightness for the front and back legs, flattening the stride and making left/right alternation hard to read.
   * **Fix:** Darken the skin and boot palette of the background (left) leg by one shade across all frames to clearly separate the stride planes.
