**Idle**
* **Weakest:** `3 down_hold` — front arm merges into the hip/skirt mass, losing negative space on the screen-right side.
* **Push:** On `2 down` and `3 down_hold`, push the screen-right (left) hand 1–2 px further down-right to keep a clear gap between arm and torso; dip the blade tip 1 px lower on `3 down_hold` for delayed weight.

**Walk**
* **Weakest:** `4 down2` — the front (screen-left) leg and blade nearly run parallel, and the free arm is completely hidden inside the torso silhouette.
* **Push:** On `3 contact2` and `4 down2`, swing the free (left) arm 2 px forward (screen-right) so it breaks out of the chest silhouette; on `2 pass`, tuck the passing foot 1 px higher to sharpen the figure-4 gap.

**Run**
* **Weakest:** `4 push2` — the back leg, sword arm, and blade compress into a single horizontal line on screen-left.
* **Push:** On `1 push` and `4 push2`, angle the blade 15–20° higher (tip up-left) to separate it from the pushing leg; on `2 air` and `5 air2`, reach the front arm 2 px further screen-right.

**Jump**
* **Weakest:** `4 apex` — both legs tuck along the same diagonal, merging into a single thick limb in the 1x silhouette.
* **Push:** On `4 apex`, scissor the legs vertically (front knee 2 px higher, back shin 2 px lower) so both boots read separately; on `1 crouch`, drop the head 1 px lower.

**Dash**
* **Weakest:** `4 recover` — legs are too close and vertical, losing the wide, braced recovery stance seen in the 2D reference.
* **Push:** On `4 recover`, step the front foot 2 px further down-right; on `3 skid`, drop the hips 1 px lower and push the braking front foot 2 px further screen-right.

**Slash**
* **Weakest:** `1 windup` — the raised sword arm and blade merge directly into the back of the head/hair silhouette.
* **Push:** On `1 windup`, pull the sword hand 2–3 px screen-left (away from the head) so negative space opens between the blade/arm and hair; on `3 impact`, drop the front knee 1 px lower.

**Overhead**
* **Weakest:** `1 raise` — torso leans too upright/back, making the windup look like a hit-recoil rather than a coiled chop.
* **Push:** On `1 raise`, shift the hips 2 px screen-left while keeping the chest arched, and angle the blade tip 2 px further back-left so it clears the hair bun cleanly.

**Hurt**
* **Weakest:** `3 recover` — stance is too narrow and upright, snapping out of the stagger without residual weight.
* **Push:** On `3 recover`, widen the feet by 2 px and tilt the head/chest 1 px forward-right; on `1 recoil`, push the sword hand 2 px lower-left to open the chest silhouette.
