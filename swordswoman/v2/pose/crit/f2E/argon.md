**Idle** (8.0/10)
- Weakest: Frame 2/3 (`down`/`down_hold`) — front thigh and skirt merge with the sword hilt, reducing negative space between the legs.
- Pushes: On frames 2–3, drop the front knee 1px screen-right and lift the sword hand 1px up to keep a clean gap above the front thigh. Flare the bow tips 1px up on frame 3 (`down_hold`) for delayed overlap.

**Walk** (7.5/10)
- Weakest: Frame 5 (`pass2`) — both legs overlap into a single vertical stump in the 1x silhouette.
- Pushes: On frames 2 and 5 (`pass`/`pass2`), drive the passing knee 2px screen-right and up so it breaks the front of the silhouette clearly. Swing the free left elbow 2px screen-left on frame 0 (`contact`) to counter-balance.

**Run** (8.5/10)
- Weakest: Frame 1 (`push`) — front bent leg tucks too close to the torso/hilt, muddying the underside silhouette.
- Pushes: On frames 1 and 4 (`push`/`push2`), drive the front knee 2px higher and screen-right. On frames 2 and 5 (`air`/`air2`), kick the trailing heel 2px higher toward the sword blade for a stronger arc.

**Jump** (8.0/10)
- Weakest: Frame 2 (`launch`) — the legs scissor too closely together, reading as one thin line at 1x.
- Pushes: On frame 2 (`launch`), split the legs by tucking the trailing knee 2px up and screen-left while extending the pushing toe 2px down-right. On frame 6 (`land`), angle the blade tip 2px lower toward the ground for heavy follow-through.

**Dash** (8.0/10)
- Weakest: Frame 3 (`skid`) — torso leans forward like the burst frames instead of fighting the forward momentum.
- Pushes: On frame 3 (`skid`), lean the torso/head 3px screen-left (backward) and plant the front heel 3px screen-right with a straight leg to sell braking. Drop the hips 2px lower on frame 1 (`burst`).

**Slash** (8.0/10)
- Weakest: Frame 1 (`windup`) — legs are nearly parallel and close together, weakening the base of support.
- Pushes: On frame 1 (`windup`), step the back foot 3px screen-left to widen the stance. On frame 3 (`impact`), lean the head/shoulders 2px further screen-right past the front knee.

**Overhead** (8.5/10)
- Weakest: Frame 3 (`impact`) — silhouette is almost identical to frame 4 (`follow`), losing the snap of impact.
- Pushes: On frame 3 (`impact`), compress the torso 2px lower over the front thigh and slam the blade tip 2px lower into the ground plane; let frame 4 (`follow`) rebound 2px up.

**Hurt** (7.5/10)
- Weakest: Frame 2 (`stagger`) — still reads too close to frame 1 (`recoil`) in silhouette, stalling the recovery rhythm.
- Pushes: On frame 1 (`recoil`), arch the spine 2px further screen-left with the head tilted back. On frame 2 (`stagger`), hunch the torso/head 3px forward (screen-right) and drop the sword tip to the floor as a crutch.
