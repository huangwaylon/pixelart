**Walk** — 8/10. Weakest: `2 pass` (passing knee, sword hilt, and front of torso clump together, hiding the knee lift in silhouette). Push: in `2 pass` and `5 pass2`, drive the passing knee 1–2 px higher and screen-right past the hilt; in `0 contact` vs `3 contact2`, tilt the blade 10° lower on `0` and 10° higher on `3` to sharpen stride alternation.

**Run** — 8/10. Weakest: `1 push` (rear shin goes horizontal and nearly touches the front heel, closing the under-body gap). Push: in `1 push` and `4 push2`, extend the pushing back leg straighter down-left (2 px further back) for a cleaner diagonal line of action; in `2 air`, kick the front foot 1 px higher.

**Jump** — 8/10. Weakest: `2 launch` (legs scissor too close together, reading as a single thick post at 1x). Push: in `2 launch` and `3 rise`, split the legs wider front-to-back (trail leg 2 px screen-left, lead knee 2 px up-right); in `1 crouch`, drop the head 1 px lower.

**Dash** — 8.5/10. Weakest: `3 skid` (front foot and low blade sit on nearly the same screen-right diagonal, competing in silhouette). Push: in `3 skid`, plant the braking front foot 2 px further screen-right and pitch the blade 20° higher so foot and sword separate cleanly.

**Slash** — 8.5/10. Weakest: `4 follow` (silhouette is nearly identical to `3 impact`, stalling the end of the swing). Push: in `3 impact`, extend the sword arm and blade 2 px further screen-right; in `4 follow`, let the torso settle 1 px back while the blade tip drops 1 px lower to touch the floor line.

**Overhead** — 8.5/10. Weakest: `1 raise` (upraised arms and sword hilt still overlap the front/top of the head silhouette). Push: in `1 raise`, pull the hands and hilt 2 px screen-left behind the head/bow so the face profile is 100% unobstructed; in `3 impact`, drop the hips 1 px lower.

**Hurt** — 8/10. Weakest: `1 recoil` (head pitches forward/down toward the knee, muddying the knockback arc compared to `0 hit`). Push: in `1 recoil`, tilt the head and upper torso 2 px up-left (arch the back into the hit) while keeping the hips low.
