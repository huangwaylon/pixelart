**Idle** (7.5/10)
- **Weakest:** f2/f3 (`down`/`down_hold`). The silhouette is almost identical to f1/f4 at 1x because the knees and free arm hold the exact same screen angles.
- **Push:** On f2/f3, drop the free left hand 1 px down-right and flare the knees 1 px wider apart so the crouch reads as a full-body settle in silhouette.

**Walk** (7.5/10)
- **Weakest:** f2 (`pass`). Despite the knee push, the left foot still touches the right ankle in the 1x silhouette, forming a closed loop/blob between the legs.
- **Push:** On f2, lift the left foot 2 px higher and 1 px further screen-right so a clean gap opens above the planted right leg. On f3 (`contact2`), extend the free left hand 1 px screen-right out from the torso.

**Run** (8.0/10)
- **Weakest:** f3 (`contact2`). The free left arm barely pokes 1 px past the chest, making the upper body silhouette boxy compared to f2.
- **Push:** On f3, punch the left arm 2 px further screen-right and drop the trailing right foot 1 px lower to separate it further from the blade.

**Jump** (8.0/10)
- **Weakest:** f3 (`rise`). The legs scissor together into a single narrow spike in silhouette, losing the two-leg read.
- **Push:** On f3, tuck the front (left) knee 2 px up-right while keeping the back leg straight down-left so the legs fork clearly at 1x.

**Dash** (8.0/10)
- **Weakest:** f1 (`burst`). The sword handle, back leg, and torso merge into one long horizontal bar on the left side.
- **Push:** On f1, angle the blade tip 2 px higher up-left so negative space opens between the blade and the trailing right leg.

**Slash** (7.5/10)
- **Weakest:** f1 (`windup`). The sword arm, blade, and head/bow merge into a single diagonal mass on the upper-left.
- **Push:** On f1, lean the head 1 px down-right and lift the sword hilt 2 px higher up-left so the blade separates from the hair/bow silhouette.

**Overhead** (7.5/10)
- **Weakest:** f2 (`smear`). The free left arm and the sword arm run parallel, merging into a thick shelf on the right side of the torso.
- **Push:** On f2–f4, sweep the free left arm back-left behind the hip so the two-handed chop down-right is unobstructed in silhouette.

**Hurt** (7.5/10)
- **Weakest:** f1 (`recoil`). The free left arm folds back into the head/hair silhouette, losing the wide flail seen in f0 and f2.
- **Push:** On f1, fling the free left arm 2 px up-right clear of the head, and tilt the head 1 px further back-left.
