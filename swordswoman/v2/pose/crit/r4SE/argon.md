**Idle** — 6.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 2 (down) — difference from Frame 0 is barely 1–2 px at 1x; legs stay too straight.
*Pushes:*
- Frames 2–3: Drop hips 2 px lower and push knees 2 px further apart (left foot left, right knee right) to widen the base.
- Frames 2–3: Dip blade tip 3 px lower-left to lag behind the body's descent.

**Walk** — 6.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 5 (pass2) — legsscissor together into a single vertical column with zero negative space.
*Pushes:*
- Frames 2 & 5 (pass): Lift the passing knee 2 px higher toward screen-right and tuck the foot back so a clear gap opens between the shins.
- Frames 0 & 3 (contact): Widen stride 2 px horizontally; swing left arm clearly out to screen-right on Frame 0.

**Run** — 7.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 3 (contact2) — reads upright and lacks the forward torso lean and arm drive of Frame 0.
*Pushes:*
- Frame 3: Pitch torso/head 2 px further lower-right to match Frame 0's forward drive.
- Frames 1 & 4 (push): Extend the pushing back leg 3 px further left-up into a straight diagonal line from head to heel.

**Jump** — 7.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 5 (fall) — silhouette is stiff and vertical, looking nearly identical to Frame 2 (launch) instead of a parachute descent.
*Pushes:*
- Frame 5: Swing blade up-left above shoulder and flare left arm up-right to create a floating V-shape.
- Frame 4 (apex): Tuck both knees 2 px higher toward the chest to compact the silhouette.

**Dash** — 7.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 3 (skid) — torso pops upright too early, losing the horizontal braking tension of the 2D reference.
*Pushes:*
- Frame 3: Keep head/chest 2 px lower, plant front foot further lower-right, and swing blade down-left across the front of the legs (matching 2D ref frame 4).
- Frame 0 (antic): Pull torso and blade 2 px further screen-left to coil before the burst.

**Slash** — 7.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 4 (follow) — blade points nearly straight down, blending with the front leg instead of extending screen-right like the 2D ref.
*Pushes:*
- Frames 3–4: Extend sword arm and blade tip 4 px further lower-right (away from the front shin) for a long horizontal reach.
- Frame 0 (antic): Twist torso so blade points across toward screen-right (matching 2D ref frame 1) for a wider swing arc.

**Overhead** — 7.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 4 (follow) — identical pose and silhouette to Frame 3 (impact), stalling the animation.
*Pushes:*
- Frame 3: Plant blade tip flat on the ground line 3 px further right.
- Frame 4: Begin rising—lift head 2 px and pull elbows back slightly to differentiate from Frame 3.

**Hurt** — 7.5/10
*Weakest:* Frame 1 (recoil) — reads like a normal standing pose rather than a mid-knockback transition between Frames 0 and 2.
*Pushes:*
- Frame 1: Arch spine back (head 2 px upper-left, hips forward) and keep left arm flung up-right.
- Frame 0: Push sword arm further down-left so the blade clears the back leg completely.
