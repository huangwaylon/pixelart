# S4_hd actions v1 (HD SDF reference model, r12 round 4)

The in-game hero body on the HD model. The GD and the animation director approved it, and it includes the round 4 Art Director blocker fix: the E/W/SE/SW ponytail is back to its legacy length and tapers to a 1-px point, and the SE rust arc no longer has a 1-px break. Fix notes: `docs/review/r12_hd_fixes_round3.md`, plus the Round 4 section of `00_REVIEW/r12_hd_model/README.md`. `releases/S4_actions_v1` is untouched; it is the rollback.

## Contents
- **13 clips:** idle, walk, run, jump_sc, fall, land, climb, climb_top, pick_up, carry_idle, carry_walk, throw, carry_throw.
- **Each clip has:**
  - `<clip>_sheet.png` and `<clip>_sheet.json` (with buildHash);
  - `_shadow.png`, `_fx_back.png` and `_fx_front.png`;
  - `_prop_front.png` / `_prop_back.png` (prop clips only);
  - `qa_report.md` (all 13 PASS, 0 warnings) and `_8dir.gif`.
- **`props/*.json`:** the prop definitions these strips were rendered with. smear and snowball are identical to v1. crate has the flattened r12 snow mound, as noted in the v1 post-release note.
- **`overrides/S4_hd/`:** the hand pixel fixes, re-applied by the pipeline. Currently there is one: `carry_idle/NW/00`, 4 px, which hides a temple-skin sliver.
- **`BUILD.json`:** buildHash per clip, plus the git base commit.

## Engine notes (GD)
1. **One set.** The HD body sheet, its `_sheet.json` and its prop strips ship together. Never pair HD bodies with v1 prop strips: the prop front/back split is re-derived from the HD body depth.
   - Prop layer tags in `throw_sheet.json` differ from v1 only at frame 0 W (back -> split). The earlier "f0 E/W, f1 E" wording was stale.
   - The other prop clips move some prop pixels between `prop_front` and `prop_back`. Total prop pixels are unchanged.
2. **Height budget.** Raise the head-clearance and occlusion-sort budget by +1 px.
   - Standing height is about 1 px taller than v1.
   - Round 3's larger bow ears move the top of the sprite 1 px higher than v1 in most clips (for example idle 22 -> 21, run N 22 -> 21). This is within the +1 px budget.
   - The horizontal extent stays within x 9-38, as in v1.
3. **Capsule collision only.** The bow loops bounce on spring chains, so collision and clearance must use a fixed capsule from the pivot (24,57), never per-frame pixel bounds.
4. **New y=59 frames.** These are lunges and landings, not locomotion; walk, run and carry_walk soles stay at y<=58.

   | Clip | v1 frames at y=59 | HD frames at y=59 |
   |---|---|---|
   | climb | 2 | 6 |
   | climb_top | 0 | 1 (NW f0) |
   | jump_sc | 22 | 24 |
   | land | 5 | 7 |
   | throw | 7 | 12 |
   | carry_throw | 5 | 9 |

5. **Shadow tint.** The light-blue shadow tint per biome is a separate ticket and is not HD-specific.

## Contract (unchanged from v1)
- frameSize 48x64, pivot (24,57), layout rows = directions, palette `frost_r3_oatmeal`.
- At most 16 colours per frame, no semi-alpha.
- Frame counts, durations, events, cancel windows, root_px / z_px, and the hand_R / hand_L / carry anchors are identical to v1.
- The shadow and fx strips are byte-identical to v1.

## Deferred to v1.1 (AD, non-blocking)
- **Throw E bow colour mix:** teal, dark teal and lavender are mixed on the bow in the release frames. These frames are transient.
- **Climb NW f0 strays:** an unoutlined hand and a cream hem blob. Climb is a low-visibility clip.

## Rebuild
```
.venv/bin/python -m pipeline.build --variant S4_hd --anim <clip> --no-reference
```
The output goes to `out/variants/S4_hd__<clip>/`. `S4_hd.json` is generated by `pipeline/tools/make_s4_hd.py`.
